# 3D film index attribution

StreamVR3D includes a small offline index of candidate 3D film titles for discovery. The index contains title names, years, identifiers, genre labels, and limited format facts. It contains no film files, streams, posters, or other artwork. A title's presence does not mean that a playable 3D copy is available or that the user has access rights.

The index was compiled using the Wikipedia contributors' [list of 3D films (1914–2004)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_3D_films_(1914%E2%80%932004)) and [list of 3D films (2005–present)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_3D_films_(2005%E2%80%93present)), together with Wikidata identifiers and genre labels. The lists were checked on 24 September 2026. The current offline index retains 538 candidates linked from the film tables on those pages. Each candidate links to the list on which it appears. Another 37 earlier candidates were set aside for individual review because neither list supplied a matching film-table link. The project selected, normalized, and combined these facts; errors or omissions are possible.
