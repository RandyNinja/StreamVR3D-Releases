# Troubleshooting and FAQ

## The installer cannot find my headset

Use a data cable and a direct USB port. Keep the headset awake and unlocked. Check Developer Mode, then accept the USB debugging prompt inside the headset. Disconnect and reconnect before choosing **Detect headset** again.

## The update is rejected

The installed app and new APK may have different signing certificates, or the APK may be older. Do not uninstall to bypass this. Report the installed version and the candidate version without sharing private keys or logs.

## Where is the app after installation?

Sideloaded apps appear under **Unknown Sources** rather than the normal store library.

## Why will a video not play?

Playback depends on the source file, codec, container, and headset decoder. DRM, Blu-ray MVC/ISO, and every audio format are not guaranteed. Test a media file you are authorized to use, then report its format without sharing the media itself.

## Is a media provider included?

No media catalogue grants access to content. Optional providers and addons may require a separate user account or configuration. Use only services and media you are authorized to access.

## Can I suggest a feature without paying?

Yes. All feature requests are free. Funding never guarantees a particular feature.

## What should I include in a report?

App version, headset model, Windows version if the installer is involved, exact steps, expected and actual result. Remove tokens, private URLs, names, and device identifiers from screenshots and logs.
